the whole thesis,
in six lines

the founders’ manifesto, kept word for word. what it means for you is written alongside it — disagree with a line and you have your answer, before any interview.

  • ai is changing the worldevery tool you use is learning the middle of the job. there is no version of a career now where this is not your problem too.
  • anyone can build, so the value lies in the distributionwhen making is cheap, getting to people is the moat. 8x organizes humans on that side of the line — that is the entire business.
  • jobs will change but never disappearthe producer becomes the director: reviewing, refining, steering the machine, carrying judgment. somebody has to hold the pen up.
  • the human in the loop becomes the most valuable and the bottlenecked resourceso we invest there. trained, supported, deployed — and treated like the constraint they are, because they are.
  • 8x supplies and orchestrates those humans250,000+ people across 50+ countries, run like software: measured, versioned, improved. a real business, not a slogan.
  • implications are global and generationalthe next decades of work are being written right now. we would rather write them with 250,000 hands than read about them.

how you’d be judged

the hiring process is the honest page — it shows what working here is like. read it, and decide fast.

step 1 of 3

pick the work

every role page lists the work, the requirements, the location, and the salary range when there is one. read it before you apply — nothing about the job waits until after.

step 2 of 3

apply, no cv

the form asks one question at a time and saves as you type. the moment you submit, a practical assignment for your role arrives — sometimes with source files attached.

step 3 of 3

show your work

send files, links, or a walkthrough video. on engineering roles the work is scored automatically and the strongest goes to a person at 8x; everywhere else, a person reads everything you send.

the assignment has a timer. the timer is tracked and never enforced.

your application stays private. your public portfolio stays yours.
